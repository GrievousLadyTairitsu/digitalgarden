# Sound
#### Sound Velocity 
###### Sound in different mediums
$V_{Solid} > V_{Liquid} > V_{Gas}$ 
###### Speed of sound in air:
$V_{Sound} = 332 + 0.6T_{c}$ where temperature is in $^\circ$ Celsius
###### Mach number and Sonic speeds
- Mach number (M)
	- $\rightarrow M = \frac{\mathrm{speed \ of \ object}}{\mathrm{speed \ of \ sound \ in \ air}} = \frac{V_o}{V_{\mathrm{sound}}}$ 
- Subsonic - V < Speed of Sound $\rightarrow$ M < 1
- Supersonic - V > Speed of Sound $\rightarrow$ M > 1
- Hypersonic M $\gt 5.0$ 
#### Sound Intensity and Level
##### Sound Intensity ($I$)
- The amount of sound energy hitting an area 1 $m^2$ in 1s ($\frac{w}{m^2}$)
- $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$, P = power, r is radius of object
##### Intensity Level (B)
- Measures Sound Intensity along a dB scale that is **logarithmic**
- $B = 10\cdot \mathrm{log}(\frac{I_1}{I_0})$ 
- $I_1$ represents the sound intensity
- $I_0$ represents the threshold of hearing (CONSTANT)
	-  $\rightarrow I_0 = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-12} \frac{w}{m^2}$ 
###### Loudness
- A completely subjective metric of perceived sound
#### Doppler effect
- $\rightarrow$ The apparent change in frequency as heard by an observer as a result of the relative motion of a source approaching an observer 
- Velocity is **conserved** $\rightarrow$ $\lambda \uparrow \ f \downarrow$ and $\lambda \downarrow f \uparrow$ 
##### Full doppler reaction
- $f_{obs} = \frac{f_s(V \pm V_0)}{V \pm V_s}$
- V = speed of sound in air
- $f_s$ is the frequency of the source (Hz)
- $V + V_0$ observer moving **towards** the source $\rightarrow V - V_0$ observer moving away from the source
- $V + V_s$ source is moving away from the source $\rightarrow$ $V - V_s$ source moving towards the source
