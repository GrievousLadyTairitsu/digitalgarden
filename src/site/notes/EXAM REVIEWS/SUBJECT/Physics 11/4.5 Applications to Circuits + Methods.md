##### Voltage
- Series: $V_t = V_1 + … + V_n$ 
- Parallel: $V_t = V_1 = … = V_n$ 
##### Current
- Series: $I_t = I_1 =…= I_n$ 
- Parallel: $I_t = I_1+…+I_n$ 
##### Resistance
- Series: $R_t = R_1 +…+R_n$ 
- Parallel: $\frac{1}{R_t} = \frac{1}{R_1} +…+ \frac{1}{R_n}$
