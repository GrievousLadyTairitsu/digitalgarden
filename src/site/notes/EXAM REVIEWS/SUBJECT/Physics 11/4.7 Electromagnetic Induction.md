# Electromagnetic Induction
### Brief Overview
- Magnetic fields cause electric current (when motion occurs such that the fields are **changing**)
- Changing magnetic fields cause a coils electric fields to be changing such that **charges are moved**
- Induces current and Voltage $\rightarrow$ faster motion, **stronger** current
##### Faradays Law
- $\epsilon_{induced} = -\frac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta t}$ , $\phi$ rep. magnetic flux
- $\Vert \epsilon_{induced} \Vert = \Vert\frac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta t}\Vert \cdot n$, n rep. number of loops
- At a instant of time: $\Vert \epsilon_{induced} \Vert = \Vert\frac{\Delta d\phi}{\Delta dt}\Vert \cdot n$ 
#### Lenz's Law
- The opposite of the **change in flux** is the induce current
	- $\rightarrow$ Induced current goes AGAINT change in flux...

#### The Ideal Transformer
- We know a changing electric current $\rightarrow$ changing magnetic field
- Thus we can use this to induce a voltage and current in a secondary coil
- Voltage can be **stepped up** or **stepped down** as needed
- In a ideal transformer, we assume that it is **100% efficient** (no energy loss)
- $\rightarrow$ Primary power = Secondary power...
