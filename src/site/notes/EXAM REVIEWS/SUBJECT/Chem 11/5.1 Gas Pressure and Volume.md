# Gas Pressure and Volume
#### Defining Pressure
- Pressure is $\frac{\vec{F}}{A}$ , which can be represented with moles and temperature for forces, and volume for the area in KMT
###### Common Units of Measurement and Statements
- **ATM** - define as normal weather at sea level
- **kPa** - defined as 1000 pascals (formal unit of pressure)
- **mmHg/Torr** - defined as millimetre of mercury
- **STP**: $\ce T = 0^\circ C, \ P = 1$ atm
- **SATP**:$\ce T = 25^\circ C, P = 100kPA$ 
###### Common Unit Conversions
- 101.325 kPA = 1 ATM = 760 mmHg
### Gas Laws
##### Boyles Laws
$\ce P_1V_1 = P_2V_2$ 
##### Charles and Gay-Lussac's Laws
$\frac{V_1}{T_1}=\frac{V_2}{T_2}$ , given Pressure is **constant**
The key difference between Charles and Lussac's is Charles requires a flexible container and Gay-Lussac a rigid one.
###### Combine Gas Law
$\frac{P_1V_1}{T_1}=\frac{P_2V_2}{T_2}$ 
This accounts for **pressure**, **volume** and **temperature** all at the same time
