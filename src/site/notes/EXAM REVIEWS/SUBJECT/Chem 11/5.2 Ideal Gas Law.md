# 5.2 Ideal Gas Law
- NOTE: **Only** applies when a gas behaves accordingly with KMT
###### Formula
$$PV = nRT$$where R is a proportionality constant
- Moles is defined as $n$ 
- Defining "R"
$$*R = 8.134 \frac{kPa \cdot L}{mol \cdot K} \ \ \ \mathrm{Or} \ \ \ R = 0.08206 \frac{atm \cdot L}{mol \cdot K}$$
NOTE: Pressure and Temperature are implied to be CONSTANT, where the volume ratio is EQUAL to the mole ratio (for all gas mixtures)
### Applications of the Ideal Gas Law
##### Molar Volume
- Molar Volume (MV) = $\frac{V}{n} = \frac{RT}{P} = MV$ 
###### Molar Mass
- $M = \frac{m}{n}$ $\rightarrow$ $M = \frac{mRT}{PV}$
- This is because $n = \frac{PV}{RT}$ $\rightarrow$ Take the inverses and you can solve for Molar Mass
###### Mass
- $m = nM$ $\rightarrow$ substitute this property into Ideal gas law
- $\rightarrow$ $m = \frac{PV}{RT}M$ 
##### Density
- $\rho = d = \frac{m}{v} = \frac{n \cdot M}{v}$, where $\rho \land d$ rep. Density
- $\rightarrow$ $\frac{n}{v} = \frac{P}{RT}$ $\rightarrow$ $\rho = \frac{PM}{RT}$ 
###### Key Ideas
- Molar Volume depends on environmental conditions only...
- Molar mass depends on the identity of the substance
- Density is **proportional** to Molar Mass
- Mass is **proportional** to Molar Mass
- Molar mass is **proportional** to mass
